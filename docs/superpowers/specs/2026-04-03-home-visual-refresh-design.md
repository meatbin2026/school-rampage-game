## Home Visual Refresh

### Goal
Make the homepage feel like a mobile arcade game instead of a product dashboard. The first screen should create immediate play intent through a stronger hero composition, less explanatory copy, and more visible motion.

### Direction
- Shift the home screen from text-first to poster-first.
- Keep the existing "热血街机校园" tone, but express it through stickers, warning tape, marquee copy, and stronger CTA treatment.
- Move explanatory content below the fold and keep the first screen focused on starting a run.

### Experience Changes
- Replace the current title card with a layered poster hero.
- Introduce short sell-point stickers instead of paragraph-heavy explanation.
- Reframe the secondary spotlight card as a punchy "出击预告" card.
- Add ambient background glow, moving stripes, a marquee strip, subtle floating motion, and CTA pulse.

### UI Principles
- One dominant action above all else.
- One-line descriptions instead of multi-line explanation wherever possible.
- More shape language: stickers, pills, badges, tape, warning labels.
- More atmosphere: light bloom, gradients, scan-like overlays, and motion.

### Scope
- Update the home screen structure.
- Update home copy to support the new composition.
- Update home-specific styles and animations.
- Preserve existing data-driven cards below the hero while reducing their visual "dashboard" feel.
